About the Therapist

Hi, I’m Ayesha Ali Khan

Psychologist & Founder of KintsugiMind

I believe therapy works best when people feel safe enough to be fully themselves — without fear of judgment or pressure to “have it all figured out.”

My approach is warm, reflective, and collaborative. Together, we explore emotional patterns, difficult experiences, relationships, and the challenges that may be affecting your wellbeing.

Whether you’re feeling anxious, emotionally stuck, overwhelmed, or simply seeking clarity, therapy can help you better understand yourself and move toward a more balanced and meaningful life.
