The Kintsugi Philosophy to Mental Health

"Kintsugi is the Japanese art of mending broken ceramics with gold alloy. At Kintsugiminds, we don't view emotional stress, academic pressure, or institutional burnout as permanent damage. We look at them as opportunities to repair individuals and cultures—making them remarkably stronger, wiser, and more beautifully adapted than they were before."
