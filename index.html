About KintsugiMind

Human, Accessible, Grounded Therapy

We believe therapy works best when people feel safe enough to be fully themselves — without fear of judgment or pressure to “have it all figured out.”

Our approach is warm, reflective, and collaborative. Together, we explore emotional patterns, difficult experiences, relationships, and the challenges that may be affecting your wellbeing.

Whether you’re feeling anxious, emotionally stuck, overwhelmed, or simply seeking clarity, our frameworks help you better understand yourself and move toward a more balanced and meaningful life.
